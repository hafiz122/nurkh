# nurkh. design direction

Reading this as: a paper-first algebra practice app for college beginners, in a modern academic visual language, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Identity

- Product mark: `nurkh.` as text, never an invented logo.
- Personality: calm, precise, encouraging, and studious.
- Primary surface: warm off-white paper.
- Ink: deep charcoal for reading and strong contrast.
- Accent: lilac-pink, reserved for notes, hints, focus, and the current learning state.
- Typography: Source Serif 4 gives problems and explanations an editorial classroom voice. IBM Plex Sans keeps controls compact and legible.

## Composition

- The equation is the focal point on the practice screen.
- Wide screens place the working prompt beside a narrow note margin. Small screens stack them in task order.
- Thin ruled details reference notebook margins without using a generic graph-paper background.
- Cards are used only for contained work surfaces, not as the default shape for every piece of information.
- Motion is limited to short state transitions after checking an answer or loading a new question.

## Decision reasons

- Warm paper reduces screen harshness and supports the physical-paper learning ritual.
- Lilac-pink notes connect hints and feedback to handwritten margin notes.
- Serif problem text separates mathematical content from interface controls.
- Restrained spacing keeps the question, paper instruction, and answer field in one visual sequence.
- A fixed light theme preserves the paper-and-ink identity.
